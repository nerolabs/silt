## 1. The mechanism, in one paragraph

The H5-B eclipse cap prices nothing **because** it keys on a self-declared label: N Sybils with N free labels are N
domains, and an undeclared Sybil is exempt (R4.3a showed capping the undeclared pool locks buckets in the default
swarm instead). This change keys the cap on **the address a peer was actually contacted at** — an opaque, per-process
salted `(class, group)` computed in the transport from a completed TLS conversation's remote address (IPv4 /24, IPv6
/32, LAN and loopback exempt) and exported through one optional `ports` interface — so the cost of an eclipse becomes
the cost of address diversity (cloud /24s at ~$3.65/mo each), the declared label is retired from the DHT entirely (the
build-immutable-#3 split, by deletion), and reply-learned IDs that were never contacted are charged to their
INTRODUCER's group (Bitcoin Core's srcgroup rule) so the address book cannot re-create the free declaration.

## 2. The shape (converged; recorded so it travels with the code)

- `ports`: `PeerClassifier` (optional) — `ClassOf(id) (class uint8, group uint64, known bool)`; classes DIRECT /
  UNVERIFIED / RELAYED. The core never sees an IP.
- `adapters/tcpnet`: on a completed handshake, `group = salted-hash(prefix(remoteAddr))`, width 24 (v4) / 32 (v6) as
  flags; loopback + RFC1918 exempt; relayed connections present the relay's remote address ⇒ class RELAYED with the
  relay's group. `adapters/simnet`: a class oracle (`SetClass`) defaulting to one group per simulated NAT box.
- `core/dht/table.go`: `SetDiversity` takes the classifier; per-bucket cap per (class, group); a table-wide cap per
  group (geth's 10-per-/24); RELAYED has its own per-relay cap and a reserve so relayed entries can never fill the
  DIRECT share; UNVERIFIED entries are charged to the introducer's group and RE-CHECKED at first classification.
- `core/node`: `domainOf` splits — the DHT reads the classifier; the C2 metric keeps the declared label.
- **SHADOW MODE first:** the veto is computed and COUNTED, not applied, behind `-dht-address-cap=shadow|on|off`
  (default shadow); five telemetry series on `/api/status`: refusals per width split direct/relayed; relay fan-in
  (clients per relay, top-relay share, Gini); placements by reachability class; PoD earnings by reachability class;
  per-bucket group-density census. Enabling `on` is an owner call against the economist's thresholds.
- **De-herd relay selection** (`cmd/silt/daemon.go` picks `KnownRelays()[0]`): least-loaded or random among known
  relays — a prerequisite the economist named; shipped here or as R4.3b's first commit.

**Decisions taken from the Tester's open list, with the reason:**

- *Which cap UNVERIFIED takes:* `min(cap_direct, cap_relay)`. An uncontacted entry is unpaid; it gets no more than the
  cheapest paid class. Under the shadow hypothesis (2/2) it is identical to either choice.
- *Loopback relay conns:* classify (RELAYED, group 0) — exempt from the per-group cap, reserve-bounded under `on`,
  unchanged under shadow. Nothing special-cased.
- *DIRECT → DIRECT re-key into a saturated group:* the re-key is applied only if the new key is admissible; otherwise the
  entry KEEPS the key it paid for. Without this, two /24s fill a bucket by shuffling (admit 2 at A and 2 at B, re-dial the
  A pair from B, admit 2 more at A — unbounded). Evicting on the move was refused: an honest peer that moved is refused-
  as-new at most, never torn out. Off and shadow always re-key, so their stored classes match each other exactly.
- *A re-introduction of an uncontacted entry by a second replier* keeps its FIRST charge in every mode — found by G-6:
  re-charging in off/shadow while `on` (through the re-key gate) kept the old charge let the three tables' stored
  classes drift, and a later refusal went uncounted.
- *`diverseNear`:* still keys on the declared label. Keying the announce/resolve SELECTION on the observed group under
  the shadow default would change production selection before the owner's `on` call, with no gate on it; the admission
  cap already bounds per-group presence in the buckets `diverseNear` reads. Deferred to the `on` enablement.
- *`SetDiversity` (the label rule):* kept in the table AND still wired by the node. It is inert against an adversary
  (that is the whole R4.3a finding) and harmless to honest operators; deleting it would rewrite the two label gates for
  no security gain. The "retire `-domain` from the DHT" line is therefore half-done by design: the cap that PRICES an
  eclipse is the address rule; the label rule is legacy.
- *Seeds:* only `-bootstrap` and `-persistent-peers` are `ObserveStatic` (cert C-2: operator-typed). `peers.json` warm-
  restart peers, DNS-seed results and mDNS finds are discovered data and go through the ordinary path, so a warm restart
  cannot exempt a cached table from the cap.
- *The transport class map* is bounded by live conns (`dropConn` deletes it, per C-4); C-3 stickiness across reconnects
  lives in the table's stored entry.
- *Reserve out of range:* the table CLAMPS into [⌈K/2⌉, K]; the daemon REFUSES the flag so the operator sees it.
