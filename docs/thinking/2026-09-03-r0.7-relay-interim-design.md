## 1. The mechanism, in one paragraph

A relay's balance rises by `chainValue` with nothing binding the chain to a real payment **because**
`SettleRelaySession` settles on the RELAY's own ledger and `RedeemRelayCredit` debits the fetcher's
fresh ephemeral, which on that ledger is a phantom auto-granted 500,000 on first touch; the anchor
specified 2026-08-27 (Q4(a)) was never built. Until R2.14 anchors the chain to a blind-signed,
relay-issued prepayment, the only honest settlement is 0: this change makes `RedeemRelayCredit` pay 0
(no anchor can be present, since no anchor type exists) and says so in the code, the flag help and
the design doc, so an operator who opts in to earn is told the lane earns nothing yet.
