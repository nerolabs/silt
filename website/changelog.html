Changelog

What changed, newest first. There is no binary release; every entry is on the main branch, and you build it from source.

27 November 2026

  • A live network you can watch: examples/live-network.sh runs validators, storage nodes, floor boxes and simulated people, and prints one link to watch them all.
  • Floor boxes follow the chain: each block a box accepts becomes its starting point for the next.
  • Publishing works through any node, not only a validator.
  • This site's source is back in the repository. The internal roadmap and changelog pages were removed, and the July pre-release binaries were withdrawn.

26 November 2026

  • Takedowns are signed by a named curator, paid for like a publish, and single-use. Operators choose which curators they follow; a node that follows none honours no on-chain takedown.
  • A floor box that falls behind, or starts from an older checkpoint, can catch up.
  • The launch validators step aside only after enough independent storage has been bonded and has stayed bonded for a sustained window.
  • A new network requires publish tokens by default, and rate-limits the free grant that pays for a newcomer's first publish.
  • Fixes for two privacy findings from the first red team.
  • A security policy, dependency and secret scans in CI, and a one-command local network.

Before November 2026, in brief

  • July. The core: files chunked, encrypted, erasure-coded and addressed by a Merkle root; a Kademlia DHT; daemons forming a real swarm; the first chain. Two early pre-releases, since withdrawn.
  • August. Proof of retrieval and space-time storage bonds; blind publish tokens, so a publish names no one; fork reconciliation; a repair economy; state roots committed in every block.
  • September. The credit and delivery economy; eclipse defences in the DHT; the floor box, a validator that keeps no chain and checks each block against proofs. Development paused on 22 September.
  • November. Resumed, with a blind red team and the fixes above.
